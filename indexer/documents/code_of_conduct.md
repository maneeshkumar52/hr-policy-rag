# Code of Conduct

**Policy Number:** HR-CC-004
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2024
**Owner:** Legal & People Operations
**Applies To:** All employees, contractors, and agency workers of Contoso Corporation

---

## 1. Purpose

Contoso Corporation is committed to conducting its business with integrity, fairness, and respect.
This Code of Conduct sets out the standards of behaviour expected of everyone who works for or on
behalf of Contoso, and helps protect both individuals and the company from harm.

---

## 2. Conflicts of Interest

Employees must avoid situations where personal interests could conflict — or appear to conflict —
with the interests of Contoso. You must disclose any potential conflict of interest to your line
manager and the Legal team as soon as it becomes apparent. Examples include:

- Holding a financial interest in a competitor or supplier
- A close family member working for a key supplier or competitor
- Undertaking paid external work that competes with Contoso's business

Undisclosed conflicts of interest may result in disciplinary action.

---

## 3. Gifts and Hospitality

Employees may accept gifts or hospitality from third parties only within the following limits:

- **Gifts:** Maximum value of **£50** per gift, per occasion
- **Hospitality:** Reasonable business meals or events (not exceeding £75/head equivalent)
- Cash or cash-equivalent gifts (gift cards, vouchers) may never be accepted, regardless of value

All gifts and hospitality above £25 must be recorded in the company's Gifts & Hospitality Register,
accessible via the company intranet. Gifts offered outside these limits must be politely declined
or, where refusal is culturally inappropriate, declared and surrendered to the office for charity
donation or disposal.

Giving gifts to public officials or government employees is strictly prohibited without prior
written approval from the Legal team, due to anti-bribery legislation.

---

## 4. Confidentiality

Employees have access to confidential information relating to Contoso's business, clients, and
colleagues. You must:

- Not disclose confidential information to any unauthorised person, inside or outside Contoso
- Treat personal data in accordance with the UK GDPR and Contoso's Data Protection Policy
- Return or securely destroy confidential materials upon leaving employment
- Not retain copies of confidential documents on personal devices without authorisation

Confidentiality obligations continue after employment ends, as set out in your employment contract.

---

## 5. Social Media Policy

Employees are personally responsible for what they post on social media. The following rules apply:

- Do not post confidential, commercially sensitive, or proprietary information about Contoso
- Do not make disparaging or defamatory comments about Contoso, its clients, or colleagues
- If identifying yourself as a Contoso employee online, make clear that views are your own
- Do not share client information, project names, or financial results without authorisation
- Harassment of colleagues or clients on social media will be treated as workplace harassment

Contoso does not routinely monitor personal social media but will investigate reports of policy breaches.

---

## 6. Workplace Harassment and Discrimination

Contoso is committed to a workplace free from harassment, bullying, and discrimination. The following
behaviours are unacceptable and may constitute gross misconduct:

- Harassment related to a protected characteristic (age, sex, race, disability, religion, sexual orientation,
  gender reassignment, pregnancy, or marital status)
- Bullying — repeated or serious negative behaviour that humiliates, undermines, or intimidates
- Sexual harassment — including unwanted physical contact, sexual comments, or inappropriate digital messages
- Victimisation of anyone who raises a concern in good faith

Employees who experience or witness harassment should report it to HR or use the anonymous
whistleblowing hotline (see Section 8). All complaints will be investigated promptly and confidentially.

---

## 7. Whistleblowing

Contoso encourages employees to raise concerns about illegal activity, health and safety risks,
financial misconduct, or serious breaches of this Code of Conduct without fear of retaliation.

You may raise concerns through:

- Your line manager or HR Business Partner
- The Legal & Compliance team directly at **compliance@contoso.com**
- The anonymous whistleblowing hotline: **0800 999 0001** (available 24/7, operated by third party)

Whistleblowers are protected under the Public Interest Disclosure Act 1998. Retaliation against an
employee for raising a concern in good faith is a disciplinary offence.

---

## 8. Disciplinary Process

Contoso operates a fair and consistent disciplinary process:

1. **Verbal warning** — for minor misconduct or first offences; documented and placed on file for 6 months
2. **Written warning** — for repeat or more serious misconduct; remains on file for 12 months
3. **Final written warning** — for serious misconduct or repeat breaches; remains on file for 24 months
4. **Dismissal** — for gross misconduct, persistent serious breaches, or where prior warnings have not led
   to improvement; with or without notice depending on circumstances

Gross misconduct (e.g. theft, fraud, serious harassment, violence) may result in summary dismissal
without prior warnings. Employees have the right to be accompanied by a colleague or trade union
representative at any formal disciplinary hearing.

---

## 9. Anti-Bribery and Corruption

Contoso operates a zero-tolerance approach to bribery and corruption. Employees must never offer,
give, request, or accept bribes. This applies to dealings with private companies and public officials.
Facilitation payments are prohibited, even where local custom may suggest otherwise.
