# Employee Expense Policy

**Policy Number:** HR-EX-003
**Effective Date:** 1 April 2024
**Owner:** Finance & People Operations
**Applies To:** All employees of Contoso Corporation UK

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs the reimbursement of business expenses incurred by employees in the course of
carrying out their duties for Contoso Corporation. All expenses must be reasonable, necessary, and
incurred wholly and exclusively for business purposes.

---

## 2. General Principles

- Expenses must be pre-approved where required (see Section 7)
- Receipts are required for all claims above £10
- Claims must be submitted within **30 calendar days** of the expense being incurred
- Personal expenses will not be reimbursed
- VAT receipts must be obtained wherever possible

---

## 3. Meals and Subsistence

The following per-meal limits apply when employees are away from their normal place of work on
business travel for more than 5 hours:

| Meal       | UK Limit | International Limit |
|------------|----------|---------------------|
| Breakfast  | £25      | £30                 |
| Lunch      | £45      | £55                 |
| Dinner     | £75      | £90                 |

Alcohol may only be included in meal claims when entertaining external clients. Alcohol consumed
without clients present is not reimbursable. Team drinks must be approved as entertainment expenses.

---

## 4. Accommodation

| Location        | Nightly Limit |
|-----------------|---------------|
| UK              | £150          |
| International   | £200          |
| London (Zone 1) | £175          |

Hotel bookings should be made through the Contoso preferred booking portal (Concur) where possible
to access negotiated rates. Serviced apartments may be used for stays exceeding 5 consecutive nights,
subject to Director approval, and must not exceed equivalent hotel rate.

---

## 5. Business Travel

### 5.1 Rail Travel

Standard class should be used for journeys under 3 hours. First class is permitted for journeys
over 3 hours when booked at least 14 days in advance, subject to line manager approval.
Rail tickets must be booked via Concur or the company's TMC (Travel Management Company).

### 5.2 Air Travel

- **Economy class** for flights of **4 hours or under** (one-way flight time)
- **Business class** permitted for flights **over 4 hours** (one-way flight time), subject to Director approval
- Premium economy may be used as an alternative to business class with Director approval
- Personal air miles may be accrued but Contoso will not pay premium fares to enable mileage collection

### 5.3 Car Travel — Mileage

Employees using their personal vehicle for business travel are reimbursed at HMRC approved mileage rates:

| Vehicle     | First 10,000 miles | Over 10,000 miles |
|-------------|--------------------|--------------------|
| Car/van     | £0.45 per mile     | £0.25 per mile     |
| Motorcycle  | £0.24 per mile     | £0.24 per mile     |
| Bicycle     | £0.20 per mile     | £0.20 per mile     |

Mileage claims must include start and end postcode and purpose of journey. Mileage to/from home to
normal place of work is not reimbursable.

### 5.4 Car Hire

Car hire must be booked via Concur. Only standard/economy class vehicles are permitted unless a
larger vehicle is required for business equipment. Employees must hold a valid UK driving licence.

### 5.5 Taxis and Ride-Sharing

Taxis may be claimed where public transport is impractical (e.g. late night travel, heavy equipment).
Uber and equivalent services are acceptable. Personal convenience is not sufficient justification.

## 6. Other Business Expenses

| Category                    | Limit         | Notes                                      |
|-----------------------------|---------------|--------------------------------------------|
| Client entertainment        | £75/head      | Requires guest list and business purpose   |
| Team entertainment          | £50/head      | Requires Director pre-approval             |
| Gifts (clients)             | £30 per gift  | No cash gifts; must be branded where possible |
| Gifts (employees)           | £50 per gift  | Birthdays/milestones, not performance-related |
| Training materials/books    | £100          | Relevant to role; no pre-approval needed   |
| Professional subscriptions  | £250/year     | Must be role-relevant; pre-approved by LM  |

---

## 7. Approval Thresholds

| Expense Amount   | Approver Required       |
|------------------|-------------------------|
| Under £500       | Line manager            |
| £500 – £2,000    | Director                |
| Over £2,000      | VP / C-Suite member     |

Pre-approval is required before incurring expenses over £500. Retrospective approval will not
normally be granted for large expenses claimed without prior authorisation.

---

## 8. Submission and Reimbursement

Expenses are submitted via **Workday** with scanned receipts attached. Claims are processed on the
15th and last business day of each month. Approved claims are reimbursed within **10 business days**
of approval via BACS to the employee's salary bank account. Late submissions may be processed in
the following payment run.

---

## 9. Non-Reimbursable Items

The following will not be reimbursed under any circumstances:

- Personal groceries or household items
- Parking fines, speeding tickets, or other penalties
- Gym memberships or personal wellness costs (see Benefits Guide)
- Personal clothing or equipment
- Costs relating to a personal vehicle's maintenance or insurance

---

## 10. Policy Breaches

Submitting fraudulent or inflated expense claims is a serious disciplinary matter and may result in
dismissal and/or referral to the police. Finance conducts random audits of expense claims.
