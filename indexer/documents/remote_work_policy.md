# Remote Work Policy

**Policy Number:** HR-RW-002
**Effective Date:** 1 March 2024
**Owner:** People & Culture Team
**Applies To:** All permanent and fixed-term employees of Contoso Corporation UK

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes Contoso Corporation's approach to remote and hybrid working, balancing
flexibility with the collaborative and operational needs of the business. It sets clear expectations
for employees working outside of a Contoso office location.

---

## 2. Eligibility

Remote working arrangements are available to employees whose roles are deemed suitable by their line
manager. Eligibility criteria include:

- Minimum 3 months' continuous service at Contoso
- Satisfactory performance rating at last review (Meets Expectations or above)
- Role is conducive to remote delivery (assessed by line manager in consultation with HR)
- Stable, secure, and appropriate home working environment

Roles requiring on-site presence (e.g. facilities, laboratory, customer-facing reception) are not
eligible for remote working arrangements.

---

## 3. Approved Work Locations

Employees may work remotely **only from locations within the United Kingdom**. Working from abroad —
including from EU member states — is not permitted without prior written approval from your Director
and the HR Business Partner. Unauthorised working from abroad may have tax, immigration, and legal
consequences for both the employee and Contoso.

Employees may not work remotely from public venues such as cafes or libraries where confidential
information could be overheard or viewed without appropriate precautions (see Section 7).

---

## 4. Hybrid Working Model

The default hybrid working pattern at Contoso is **3 days in office, 2 days remote** per week.
Teams may agree different arrangements with their Director, subject to business requirements.
Individual hybrid patterns must be agreed in writing with the line manager and stored in the HR system.

---

## 5. Equipment Provision

### 5.1 Standard Equipment

Contoso will provide all employees approved for remote work with:

- A company-managed laptop (Windows or MacOS as role requires)
- A company mobile phone or softphone application where required by role

### 5.2 Home Office Allowance

Employees are entitled to a **one-time home office setup allowance of £500** to cover furniture
and peripherals (e.g. external monitor, keyboard, mouse, desk chair). Claims must be submitted
via Workday within 90 days of the remote work arrangement being approved, with receipts attached.
The allowance is non-contractual and subject to annual review.

---

## 6. Internet and Utilities Allowance

Employees working remotely are entitled to a **monthly internet contribution of £25**, paid as
a non-pensionable addition to salary. This is intended to contribute toward home broadband costs.
The allowance is subject to tax and NI in the usual way and will appear as a separate line on payslips.

---

## 7. Core Hours and Availability

All remote employees must be available and contactable during **core hours of 10:00am – 3:00pm
Monday to Friday** (excluding bank holidays). Outside core hours, employees may flex their schedule
by agreement with their line manager.

Employees must have their work status clearly indicated in Microsoft Teams during working hours.

---

## 8. Security Requirements

The following security measures are **mandatory** for all remote workers:

- **VPN:** All company systems must be accessed via the Contoso VPN at all times when working remotely
- **Screen lock:** Laptops must be locked when leaving the desk (Windows Key + L / Cmd + Ctrl + Q)
- **Wi-Fi:** Only password-protected home or corporate Wi-Fi networks may be used
- **Clean desk:** No confidential documents should be left visible when not in use
- **Video calls:** Be mindful of what is visible in your background during video calls

Any security incidents must be reported immediately to the IT Security team at **security@contoso.com**.

---

## 9. Line Manager Approval Process

1. Employee completes the **Remote Work Request Form** in Workday
2. Line manager reviews the request within 10 business days
3. If approved, line manager updates the employee's work location record in Workday
4. HR Business Partner is notified automatically and will confirm the arrangement in writing
5. Arrangements are reviewed at least annually during the performance review cycle

Requests may be declined where business needs cannot be met remotely. Employees may appeal
decisions through the standard grievance process.

---

## 10. Health and Safety

Employees working from home are responsible for ensuring their home workspace meets basic DSE
(Display Screen Equipment) standards. A self-assessment DSE checklist must be completed in
Workday before commencing remote work. Contoso may arrange a visit by an occupational health
assessor if concerns are identified.

---

## 11. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact **hr@contoso.com** or speak with your HR Business Partner.
