# Employee Benefits Guide

**Document Number:** HR-BG-005
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2024
**Owner:** People & Culture Team
**Applies To:** All permanent employees of Contoso Corporation UK

---

## 1. Introduction

Contoso Corporation is committed to supporting the health, financial security, and wellbeing of our
employees. This guide provides an overview of the benefits available to you as a permanent employee.
Most benefits are available from your first day of employment unless stated otherwise.

---

## 2. Private Health Insurance

Contoso provides **BUPA private health insurance** for all permanent employees, with the option to
extend cover to family members at a subsidised rate.

- **Employee cover:** Fully funded by Contoso (no employee contribution)
- **Family plan (partner + children):** Available at 40% of the commercial rate, deducted monthly via payroll
- Cover includes: private consultations, diagnostic tests, inpatient and day-patient treatment,
  mental health support, physiotherapy (up to 10 sessions per year), and outpatient care
- **Dental and optical:** Covered separately (see Section 5)
- Enrolment: Complete the BUPA enrolment form via Workday within 30 days of joining

---

## 3. Pension Scheme

Contoso operates a workplace pension through **Legal & General** under auto-enrolment legislation.

- **Employer contribution:** 5% of qualifying earnings (matched up to employee contribution of 5%)
- **Minimum employee contribution:** 3% (auto-enrolled default)
- **Maximum employer match:** 5% (if employee contributes 5% or more)
- Contributions above 5% are permitted but receive no additional employer match
- The pension scheme is a **defined contribution** scheme; funds are invested in a default lifestyle
  fund unless the employee selects an alternative investment option
- Employees may opt out within 30 days of enrolment by contacting Legal & General directly

---

## 4. Life Assurance

Contoso provides **Group Life Assurance** at **4 times annual salary** (4x), paid as a tax-free
lump sum to your nominated beneficiary in the event of death in service.

- Cover begins on your first day of employment
- Nomination of beneficiary form must be submitted to HR within 30 days of joining
- Beneficiary designations can be updated at any time via HR
- Cover ends upon leaving Contoso employment or reaching age 70, whichever is earlier

---

## 5. Dental Cover

All employees receive access to **Denplan dental cover**, funded by Contoso:

- Routine check-ups: 2 per year, fully covered
- Hygienist visits: 2 per year, fully covered
- Restorative treatment (fillings, extractions): up to £500 per year covered
- Orthodontic treatment: not included in company plan (available via BUPA dental upgrade at own cost)
- Register with a Denplan-registered dentist and quote your Contoso membership number

---

## 6. Annual Leave

Employees are entitled to **28 days of paid annual leave per year**, in addition to UK public
and bank holidays (typically 8 days). This gives a total entitlement of **36 days**.

- Leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December
- Up to **5 days may be carried over** to the following leave year; carried-over leave must be taken
  by 31 March
- Employees may **buy up to 5 additional days** of annual leave per year via the flexible benefits
  portal, subject to payroll deadlines (elections made each November for following year)
- Holiday requests should be submitted via Workday and are subject to line manager approval

---

## 7. Cycle to Work Scheme

Contoso participates in the government-approved **Cycle to Work** scheme, enabling employees to
purchase a bicycle and safety equipment through a salary sacrifice arrangement, saving on income tax
and National Insurance.

- Maximum value: **£2,000** (standard) or **£5,000** (e-bikes and cargo bikes)
- Minimum repayment period: 12 months via salary sacrifice
- Eligible items: bicycles, helmets, lights, locks, waterproofs, and panniers
- Apply via the Cycle Solutions portal, accessible through the Contoso benefits portal

---

## 8. Season Ticket Loans

Employees commuting by public transport may apply for an **interest-free season ticket loan** to
cover the cost of annual rail, tube, or bus season tickets.

- Maximum loan value: **£10,000**
- Repaid monthly via payroll over 10 months
- Available after completing 3 months of service
- In the event of resignation, the outstanding balance is deducted from the final salary payment
- Apply via Workday with a copy of the season ticket quotation

---

## 9. Employee Assistance Programme (EAP)

Contoso provides access to a confidential **Employee Assistance Programme** (EAP) operated by
Health Assured, available to employees and their immediate household members.

Services include:

- **24/7 counselling helpline:** Free, confidential telephone support — call **0800 028 0199**
- **Structured counselling:** Up to 8 sessions of face-to-face or video counselling per year
- **Legal advice:** Free 30-minute telephone consultations on personal legal matters
- **Financial guidance:** Access to independent financial advice
- **Wellbeing resources:** Online CBT tools, mindfulness sessions, and wellbeing articles

All EAP services are completely confidential; Contoso does not receive information about individual usage.

## 11. Flexible Benefits Portal

Most benefits can be managed via the **Contoso Flexible Benefits Portal** at benefits.contoso.com.
Annual elections are made each November for the following calendar year. Mid-year changes are
permitted only for qualifying life events (marriage, birth of child, change in circumstances).
