# Parental Leave Policy

**Policy Number:** HR-PL-001
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2024
**Owner:** People & Culture Team
**Applies To:** All permanent employees of Contoso Corporation UK

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out Contoso Corporation's approach to parental leave, ensuring all eligible employees
can take time away from work to care for a new child, in line with or exceeding UK statutory requirements.

---

## 2. Maternity Leave

### 2.1 Entitlement

All pregnant employees are entitled to up to **52 weeks of maternity leave**, regardless of length of service.
This is divided into:

- **Ordinary Maternity Leave (OML):** First 26 weeks
- **Additional Maternity Leave (AML):** Remaining 26 weeks

Maternity leave may begin no earlier than 11 weeks before the expected week of childbirth.

### 2.2 Statutory Maternity Pay (SMP)

Employees who have 26 weeks' continuous service by the 15th week before the expected week of childbirth
and earn at or above the Lower Earnings Limit (LEL) are entitled to **39 weeks of Statutory Maternity Pay (SMP)**:

- **Weeks 1–6:** 90% of average weekly earnings (no cap)
- **Weeks 7–39:** The lesser of 90% of average weekly earnings or the statutory weekly rate (£184.03 as of April 2024)
- **Weeks 40–52:** Unpaid

Contoso enhances SMP to **full pay for the first 16 weeks**, then SMP rate for weeks 17–39.

---

## 3. Paternity Leave

### 3.1 Entitlement

Eligible employees (partner of the mother/primary adopter) may take up to **2 weeks of paternity leave**,
taken as either one continuous week or two separate weeks, within 56 days of the child's birth or placement.

### 3.2 Statutory Paternity Pay (SPP)

Employees eligible for SPP will receive the lower of 90% of average weekly earnings or the statutory
weekly rate (£184.03). Contoso enhances this to **full pay for both weeks**.

---

## 4. Shared Parental Leave (SPL)

### 4.1 Overview

Eligible couples may share up to **50 weeks of leave and 37 weeks of pay** between them following the
birth or adoption of a child. The mother/primary adopter must end their maternity/adoption leave early
to trigger SPL.

### 4.2 Eligibility

Both parents must:

- Be employed or self-employed
- Have at least 26 weeks' continuous service by the end of the 15th week before the expected week of childbirth
- Meet the earnings threshold (currently £123/week)

### 4.3 Pay During SPL

Contoso pays **Shared Parental Pay (ShPP)** at the statutory rate for eligible weeks. Employees who
utilise SPL within the first 16 weeks following birth/placement will receive Contoso's enhanced rate
equivalent to full pay.

---

## 5. Adoption Leave

Employees who adopt a child are entitled to adoption leave and pay on the same terms as maternity
leave, provided they are the primary adopter. Secondary adopters are entitled to paternity leave.

---

## 6. Keeping in Touch (KIT) Days

Employees on maternity or adoption leave may work up to **10 Keeping in Touch days** without ending
their leave or affecting their SMP. SPL employees may each take up to **20 Shared Parental Leave
in Touch (SPLIT) days**. KIT days are paid at normal daily rate and agreed in advance with line manager.

---

## 7. Return to Work

Employees returning from OML (first 26 weeks) are entitled to return to **the same job** on the same
terms and conditions. Employees returning from AML are entitled to return to the same job or, if not
reasonably practicable, a suitable alternative role on no less favourable terms.

Flexible working requests submitted on return will be considered in line with the Flexible Working Policy.

---

## 8. Notification Requirements

- Maternity leave: notify HR and line manager at least **15 weeks before expected week of childbirth**
- Paternity leave: notify HR at least **15 weeks before** the expected birth date
- SPL: written notice at least **8 weeks before** the first SPL period
